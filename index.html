About Patel Industries

From a Rural Village to a Vision-Driven Enterprise

Patel Karkhana Group Pvt. Ltd. represents the spirit of self-reliance — a company born in a small village with nothing but determination.

Who We Are

Patel Karkhana Group Pvt. Ltd. was established on 3rd March 2019 in the rural heartland of Nawaibil, District West Karbi Anglong, Assam. What began as a student's bold dream has grown into a company driven by purpose and innovation.

Today, the company focuses on two powerful pillars — Technology and Education — aiming to bridge the gap between rural talent and modern opportunity.

Our Story

A Journey That Started With Just ₹50

Every great company has a beginning. Ours started with a laptop, fifty rupees, and an unshakable belief.

3rd March 2019

The Beginning

Patel Karkhana Group Pvt. Ltd. was officially established in Nawaibil, West Karbi Anglong, Assam — a rural area with limited resources but unlimited potential.

The Founder's Leap

A 21-Year-Old Student's Dream

The company was founded by a young student who was only 21 years old at the time. With no financial backing and no safety net, he chose to build instead of wait.

Starting Capital

Just ₹50 and a Laptop

When the company began, the founder had only ₹50 and a laptop. That was it. No investors, no office, no team — just an idea and the courage to start.

Today

Focused on Technology & Education

Today, Patel Karkhana Group Pvt. Ltd. is working to create meaningful impact in technology and education — empowering people and building solutions for a better tomorrow.

The Visionary

One Student. One Laptop. One Big Dream.
